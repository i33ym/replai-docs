## Overview

Connect your YouTube channel to Replai to automatically sync videos, manage comments with AI, and grow your audience engagement.

---

## How to Connect

### Step 1: Add Account from Sidebar

In the Replai dashboard, locate the **Accounts** section on the left-hand sidebar. Click the **+ Add account** button.

![Add account sidebar](/images/youtube/01-sidebar-add-account.png)

### Step 2: Select YouTube

A menu will appear listing the supported social platforms. Select **YouTube** from the list.

![Select YouTube platform](/images/youtube/02-select-youtube-platform.png)

### Step 3: Authorize via Google

A popup window will appear. Click the **Continue with Google** button to open the secure Google authorization page.

![Connect YouTube popup](/images/youtube/03-connect-popup.png)

1. **Choose your account**: Select the Gmail/Google account that owns your YouTube channel.
2. **Confirm Permissions**: Review the access request and click **Allow**.

![Google Login](/images/youtube/04-google-oauth-login.png)

### Step 4: Select Your Channel

If your Google account manages multiple channels or Brand Accounts, select the specific channel you wish to connect and click **Finish Connection**.

![Select YouTube channel](/images/youtube/05-select-channel-list.png)

---

## What Happens Next

Once connected, Replai automatically begins the following processes:

1. **Video Syncing** - We fetch your public and private videos to prepare for comment management.
2. **Comment Monitoring** - The system checks for new comments every 10 minutes.
3. **AI Suggestion Engine** - Replai starts generating high-quality response suggestions based on your video context.

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid or Expired Token"
**Problem:** Your session has timed out or permissions were revoked.
**Solution:** Click the "Disconnect" icon in your account settings and follow the **3-step connection process** again to refresh the token.

### "No YouTube Channel Found"
**Problem:** The selected Google account doesn't have an active channel.
**Solution:** Go to [YouTube](https://youtube.com), ensure a channel is created for that account, then return to Replai.

## Privacy & Security

- ✅ **No Passwords**: We use official Google OAuth. We never see your password.
- ✅ **Limited Access**: Replai only accesses YouTube data. We cannot read your Emails or Drive files.
- ✅ **Revocable**: You can remove Replai's access at any time via your [Google Security Dashboard](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## FAQ

**Q: Can I connect multiple YouTube channels?** A: Yes! Simply repeat the "Add Account" process for each channel.

**Q: Does Replai support Unlisted videos?** A: To keep your workspace focused, we filter out Unlisted videos by default. Public and Private videos are supported.
