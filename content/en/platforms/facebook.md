## Overview

Connect your Facebook Page to Gapex to manage comments on your posts with AI, reply to your audience, and improve engagement.

---

## Requirements

Before connecting, make sure you have the following:

- A Facebook **Page** (Business or Community)
- You must be an **Admin** of that Page

> Personal profiles cannot be connected. Only Facebook Pages are supported.

---

## 2. Connect to Gapex

### Step 8: Sign in to Gapex

Go to [gapex.ai](https://gapex.ai) and sign in to your account.

### Step 9: Add account

Go to the **Accounts** section in the left panel and click the **+ Add account** button.

### Step 10: Select Facebook

Select **Facebook** from the list of platforms and click **Continue with Facebook**.

### Step 11: Authorize via Facebook

The Facebook login page will open:

1. **Log in to Facebook**: Enter your email and password and sign in
2. **Confirm permissions**: Gapex will request the following permissions:
   - View Page posts
   - Manage Page comments
   - Read Page information
3. Review the permissions and click **Continue**

### Step 12: Select your Page

A list of Facebook Pages you manage will appear. Select the Page you want to connect and click **Connect**.

### Step 13: Done!

Your Facebook Page has been successfully connected to Gapex. You can now view your posts and comments on the dashboard.

---

## What happens next

After connecting, Gapex automatically starts the following processes:

1. **Post sync** — Your Facebook Page posts appear in the Gapex dashboard.
2. **Comment monitoring** — The system checks for new comments every 5 minutes.
3. **AI suggestion system** — Gapex generates response suggestions based on post context.

## Settings

Configure Page behavior in **Account Settings**:

### Auto-response settings
* **Enable auto-reply**: Automatically post AI responses that match your confidence score.
* **Review before posting**: Hold responses for manual approval before they go live.

### Comment filters
* **Comment filter**: Sync all comments or only unanswered ones.

## Troubleshooting

### "Facebook Page not showing"
**Problem:** No Pages appear in the selection screen.
**Solution:** Make sure you are an **Admin** of the Page. Only users with Admin permissions can connect a Page.

### "Comments not appearing"
**Problem:** No comments visible in the dashboard.
**Solution:** Comments sync every **5 minutes**. Wait a moment. If a post has no comments, it will appear empty.

### "Invalid or Expired Token"
**Problem:** Your session has timed out or permissions were revoked.
**Solution:** Click **Disconnect** in Account Settings and follow the connection process again.

---

## Privacy & Security

* **No passwords needed**: We use official Facebook OAuth. We never see your password.
* **Limited access**: Gapex only accesses your Facebook Page data. We cannot access your personal profile.
* **Revocable**: You can remove Gapex's access at any time via Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I connect multiple Facebook Pages?**
A: Yes! Simply repeat the "Add Account" process for each Page.

**Q: Can I delete comments through Gapex?**
A: Yes, you can view, reply to, hide, and delete comments through Gapex.

**Q: How do I disconnect a Page?**
A: Go to Settings and click the **Disconnect** button next to the account.

---

## Limitations

- **Admin required**: Only Page Admins can connect an account.
- **Sync interval**: Comments sync every 5 minutes, not in real-time.
- **Personal profiles**: Personal Facebook profiles cannot be connected, only Pages.
