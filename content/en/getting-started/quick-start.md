## Overview

This guide walks you through the steps to get your first AI response in Gapex. The process takes less than 5 minutes.

---

## 1. Create an account

1. Go to [gapex.ai](https://gapex.ai)
2. Click the **Sign Up** button
3. Enter your name, email and password (confirm password twice) or sign up quickly via **Google/Facebook**
4. Enter the verification code sent to your email on the platform
5. Sign in with your email and password

> If you already have an account, simply sign in.

---

## 2. Connect a platform

After signing in, connect your first social media account:

1. Go to the **Accounts** section in the left panel
2. Click the **+ Add account** button
3. Select a platform (e.g., **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **YouTube**, etc.)
4. Follow the instructions to authorize access

## 3. Explore the dashboard

After connecting a platform, the Gapex dashboard consists of the following sections:

### Main panel
- **Comments/Reviews** — comments from all platforms in one place
- **AI suggestions** — AI-generated response suggestions for each comment
- **Statistics** — count of responded, pending, and skipped comments

### Left panel
- **Dashboard** — overview
- **Accounts** — connected accounts
- **Settings** — platform settings

---

## 4. Get your first AI response

1. View new comments on the **Dashboard**
2. Click on a comment
3. Gapex automatically shows an AI response suggestion
4. Review the response:
   - **Post** — post the response directly
   - **Edit** — modify the response before posting
   - **Reject** — request a different suggestion
5. Once posted, the response automatically appears on the respective platform

---

## 5. Configure response rules (optional)

You can customize Gapex to fit your needs:

### AI tone settings
In **Settings → AI → Tone**, select a response style:
- **Formal** — for business and corporate accounts
- **Friendly** — for brands and personal accounts
- **Neutral** — universal style

### Auto-response
In **Settings → AI → Auto-response**:
- Automatically post AI responses or work in review mode
- Set a confidence threshold (e.g., auto-post when above 85%)

### Comment filters
In **Settings → Filters**:
- Sync all comments or only unanswered ones
- Filter by specific keywords
